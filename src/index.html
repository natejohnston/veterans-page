Questions? We’ve got answers.

What information do I need?

You’ll need your service details, estimated purchase price, down payment amount, and ZIP code. The whole process takes about 3 minutes.

What happens after I submit?

A licensed VU loan specialist will reach out to walk through your personalized options—no pressure and no obligation.

How is a VA loan different from a conventional loan?

VA loans can offer no down payment, no private mortgage insurance, and competitive rates to eligible Veterans, service members, and surviving spouses.

Does this affect my credit?

No. Your initial quote uses a soft credit inquiry, which does not affect your credit score.

How long does this process take?

Many Veterans close in 30 days or less. Your loan specialist will provide a timeline based on your situation.
